FIELD LIBRARY

Verification toolkit.

Short reference cards you can consult before, during, or after a mission.

⌕
SOURCE CHECK

Trace the original

Find who first published the claim and whether that person or organization has direct knowledge.

  • Open the original page
  • Check author and date
  • Compare independent sources
◫
VISUAL CHECK

Restore context

An authentic image can still mislead when paired with a false place, date, or explanation.

  • Search earlier appearances
  • Inspect caption and crop
  • Confirm location and event
⌁
AI CHECK

Audit generated answers

Treat AI output as material to verify, not as evidence by itself.

  • Flag absolute language
  • Verify every citation
  • Disclose assistance
⚿
SCAM CHECK

Protect your data

Urgency, unexpected rewards, and requests for credentials are common manipulation tactics.

  • Do not open unknown links
  • Use official channels
  • Never share passwords
◌
DIALOGUE CHECK

Respond responsibly

Correct harmful content without attacking people or escalating the conflict.

  • Focus on the claim
  • Provide evidence
  • Protect affected users
♜
FINAL CHECK

Evidence hierarchy

Prefer direct, transparent, current evidence over anonymous summaries or viral popularity.

  • Primary documentation
  • Independent confirmation
  • Known limitations
